## 2. What the app is

Insignia is an **educational reference** about the Swiss Armed Forces: ranks, branches, functions, and badges, a quiz, and a guide for military service (e.g. RS countdown, packing list, rights and duties, pay calculator, abbreviations, career paths, radio alphabet).

**Unofficial app:** Insignia is a private project. It is **not** an official app of the Swiss Armed Forces, the Federal Department of Defence, Civil Protection and Sport (DDPS / VBS), or any other authority, and is not endorsed by them. Official information is available at [armee.ch](https://www.armee.ch) and from the responsible authorities.

## 3. No guarantee of accuracy

We put care into the content and name our sources, but we **cannot guarantee** that everything is complete, correct, or up to date. Rules, amounts, and dates can change at any time.

- **Pay and EO calculator:** results are **estimates without guarantee**. The amounts actually paid are set by the Armed Forces, the compensation fund, and your employer.
- **RS dates, countdown, and notifications:** what counts is the date on your **marching orders**, not the date in the app.
